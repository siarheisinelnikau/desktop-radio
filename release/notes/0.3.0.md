Desktop Radio 0.3.0 adds a compact player, makes the station list easier to navigate, and improves the opening animation and audio visualization. Windows 10 and 11 (x64) users can install it from Settings in Desktop Radio or download the standalone `DesktopRadio-v0.3.0.exe` below. In-app installation restarts the player and preserves its settings.

### Added

- Collapse the station list to keep a compact player with playback controls, volume, and the audio visualization. The animated panel remembers its last layout and opens in its own window when compact.
- Start Desktop Radio in the notification area when you sign in to Windows. This is enabled by default and can be switched off separately from resuming the last station.
- Open station search from the magnifying glass or with Ctrl+F. The search field slides open, and closing it makes room for a seventh station row.

### Improved

- The opening animation uses the Android broadcast mast and signal rings, with an iris reveal into the player. The covered first frame prevents the player from briefly appearing before the intro.
- The live spectrum and waveform update more smoothly. Audio visualization redraws its own area, and the window stops repainting while hidden in the tray.
- Drag the scrollbar thumb in the player and station management. The wheel follows the Windows scrolling preference and counts partial wheel steps.

### Fixed

- Selecting the currently playing or connecting station keeps the existing stream. Selecting it after a pause or failure starts playback again.
- The compact transition keeps the toolbar sharp, hides the full-size window, and draws the outline around the compact player.
- The Manage stations button has its own row beneath Updates.
